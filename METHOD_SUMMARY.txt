CMGT-DINO components
====================

Visual branch
-------------
Frozen DINOv3 ViT-S/16.
Early / middle / late stage embeddings.
E->M, M->L and E->L embedding change vectors.

Phenotype branch
----------------
Stage statistics and longitudinal changes for Horti-M3 phenotype variables.

Environment branch
------------------
Stage environmental statistics plus VPD, growing-degree-hour,
high-temperature-hour, light exposure proxy and CO2 exposure proxy.

Cross-Modal Growth Concordance
------------------------------
Pairwise growth-trajectory agreement between:
- image and phenotype,
- image and environment,
- phenotype and environment.

Final head
----------
OOF-validated lightweight candidate regression heads with an optional
non-negative OOF blend, accepted only when OOF RMSE improves.

Evaluation
----------
20 year-stratified repeated held-out evaluations.
Leave-one-year-out validation.
Backbone ablation.
Ablation and statistical significance tests.
